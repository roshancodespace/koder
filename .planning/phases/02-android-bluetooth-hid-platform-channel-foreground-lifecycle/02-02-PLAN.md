<objective>
Implement the Android 14+ compliant foreground service (CompanionForegroundService) in companion-app/ with foregroundServiceType='connectedDevice' and persistent notification management. Prevent background process freezing and premature Bluetooth HID disconnection during active companion sessions, wire platform channel controls to a Dart ForegroundServiceManager, and integrate status indication into the primary companion UI.

Purpose: Guarantees that the Android OS does not terminate the companion session or tear down Bluetooth HID registration when the user switches apps or the device screen dims.
